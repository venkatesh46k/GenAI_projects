# Domestic Roaming Charges

Domestic roaming means using the mobile service on another operator's network area within India, outside the subscriber's home circle. This document covers how it is rated for prepaid subscribers.

## Summary by plan

| Plan | Incoming calls | Outgoing calls | SMS | Data |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PLAN_199 (Basic) | Free | Counted from the bundled 1000 minutes | Counted from the 100 SMS per day | Counted from the daily 1.5 GB |
| PLAN_599 (Premium) | Free | Counted from the bundled 3000 minutes | Counted from the 100 SMS per day | Counted from the daily 2.5 GB |
| PLAN_99 (Data top-up) | Not applicable | Not applicable | Not applicable | Adds 1 GB per day, usable while roaming |

## Rules

- **Incoming calls are free** on domestic roaming for all plans with active validity.
- **Outgoing calls** while roaming consume the same bundled minutes as at home. There is no separate roaming surcharge while bundled minutes remain.
- **Out-of-bundle usage** while roaming is rated at the standard out-of-bundle tariff from the main balance. A roaming premium of ₹0.50 per minute applies to outgoing voice beyond the bundle on the Basic plan only. The Premium plan does not carry this premium.
- **Data** while roaming uses the same daily quota as at home.
- **National roaming SMS** to any Indian number follows the same daily SMS limit.

## International roaming

International roaming is not part of any plan described here. It requires a separate activation and is rated at international tariffs. This assistant does not have international rates, and the subscriber should contact customer care.

## Roaming disputes

If a subscriber believes they were charged a roaming premium while minutes were still available in the bundle, they should raise a billing dispute. The CDR for the call will show the rated charge and can be checked against the plan allowance (see the CDR specification).
