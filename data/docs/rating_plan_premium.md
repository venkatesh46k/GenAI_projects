# Premium 599 Plan (PLAN_599)

The Premium 599 plan is the long-validity, high-allowance prepaid pack for heavy data users and frequent callers.

## Plan summary

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Plan ID | PLAN_599 |
| Price | ₹599 |
| Validity | 56 days |
| Data | 2.5 GB per day |
| Voice | 3000 minutes total for the validity period |
| SMS | 100 SMS per day |

## Benefits and limits

- **Data:** 2.5 GB is credited every day at midnight, with no carry-forward of unused data. After the daily quota is used, speed drops to 64 kbps until the next midnight.
- **Voice:** 3000 minutes shared across the 56-day validity for local and national calls. Once exhausted, calls are charged from the main balance at the out-of-bundle rate.
- **SMS:** 100 SMS per day, local and national.
- **Validity:** 56 days from the date and time of successful recharge, double the Basic 199 plan.

## Extra benefits

- **Priority customer care:** Subscribers on PLAN_599 are routed to the priority queue when they raise a billing dispute, and their disputes carry a shorter resolution target (see the dispute resolution SOP).
- **Domestic roaming:** Incoming and outgoing calls while roaming within India are covered by the bundle at no extra charge, unlike the Basic plan where roaming outgoing calls may be rated separately (see the roaming charges document).
- **Balance protection:** The main balance is not used for data while the daily quota has not been exhausted.

## Buying the plan

Recharge with ₹599 and select PLAN_599. Recharging again with the same plan before expiry extends validity by 56 days from the current expiry date.

## Comparison with Basic 199

PLAN_599 costs about 3 times as much as PLAN_199 but gives 2 times the validity, 1.67 times the daily data and 3 times the voice minutes, so the effective cost per day is lower (roughly ₹10.70 per day versus ₹7.10 per day for the Basic plan, with a much larger allowance).
