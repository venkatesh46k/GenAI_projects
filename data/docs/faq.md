# Frequently Asked Questions

**1. How do I check my balance?**
Ask the assistant with your 10-digit mobile number, for example "What is the balance for 9876543210?". It shows your main balance, current plan and account status.

**2. What plans are available?**
Basic 199 (₹199, 28 days, 1.5 GB per day, 1000 minutes, 100 SMS per day), Premium 599 (₹599, 56 days, 2.5 GB per day, 3000 minutes, 100 SMS per day) and the Data Topup 99 (₹99, 30 days, 1 GB per day, data only).

**3. How long is my plan valid?**
Validity starts from the recharge time: 28 days for Basic 199, 56 days for Premium 599, 30 days for Data Topup 99.

**4. Does unused daily data carry forward?**
No. Daily data resets at midnight and unused data does not carry to the next day.

**5. What happens when my daily data is finished?**
Speed is reduced to 64 kbps until midnight, when the daily quota is credited again.

**6. What happens when my plan expires?**
You have a 15-day grace period in which incoming calls and SMS continue. Recharging restores full service immediately.

**7. Why was I charged even though I have an active plan?**
The daily data or SMS limit may have been used up, minutes may be exhausted, or the usage was outside the bundle. Check the CDR for the call. If you think it is wrong, raise a billing dispute.

**8. How do I raise a billing dispute?**
Tell the assistant what happened, for example "I was charged twice for my recharge". A dispute ID such as D-100001 is created.

**9. How long does a dispute take?**
About 7 working days for standard plans and 3 working days for the Premium 599 plan. Escalated cases take up to 10 working days.

**10. What if I am not satisfied?**
You can ask to escalate. A human specialist reviews the case and you receive a ticket ID such as TCK-123abc.

**11. My recharge was debited but the balance did not change. What do I do?**
Wait a short time and check the balance again. If it is still not credited, raise a dispute. Money that was not credited is reversed within 7 working days.

**12. Will my number be barred if my balance is low?**
Below ₹5, outgoing calls and out-of-bundle usage are barred. Recharging removes the barring immediately.

**13. Do incoming calls cost anything while roaming in India?**
No. Incoming calls are free on domestic roaming.

**14. Can I buy a data pack without a base plan?**
The top-up data works only while a base plan is active.

**15. How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are issued after a dispute is verified. Wrong deductions are credited back to your balance; failed recharges are reversed to your payment source within 7 working days.

**16. Is international roaming included?**
No. It needs a separate activation and separate tariffs.
