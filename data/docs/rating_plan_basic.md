# Basic 199 Plan (PLAN_199)

The Basic 199 plan is the entry-level monthly prepaid pack. It suits subscribers with moderate daily data needs and regular calling.

## Plan summary

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Plan ID | PLAN_199 |
| Price | ₹199 |
| Validity | 28 days |
| Data | 1.5 GB per day |
| Voice | 1000 minutes total for the validity period |
| SMS | 100 SMS per day |

## Benefits and limits

- **Data:** 1.5 GB is credited every day at midnight. Unused daily data does not carry forward to the next day. After the daily 1.5 GB is used, speed is reduced to 64 kbps until the next midnight.
- **Voice:** 1000 minutes are shared across the whole validity period, for local and national calls. When the minutes are used up, calls are charged at the out-of-bundle rate from the main balance.
- **SMS:** 100 SMS per day, local and national. Additional SMS are charged per message from the main balance.
- **Validity:** 28 days starting from the date and time of a successful recharge. It is not a calendar month, so the plan renews roughly every 4 weeks.

## Buying the plan

Recharge with exactly ₹199 and select PLAN_199. The recharge amount is added to the subscriber's balance and the plan benefits are activated. If the subscriber recharges again before validity ends with the same plan, the validity is extended by another 28 days from the current expiry.

## What is not included

- International calls and international roaming.
- Premium-rate numbers and value-added services.
- Data top-up packs, which are sold separately (see the data top-up document).

For what happens when the plan lapses, refer to the plan validity and expiry document.
